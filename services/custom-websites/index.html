COMMON QUESTIONS

Before You
Say Yes.

These are some of the questions that naturally come up before a custom website project begins.

How much does a custom website cost?+

Scope depends on the number of pages, content, functionality, integrations and complexity. We recommend the right-sized solution after understanding what the website needs to accomplish rather than forcing every business into the same package.

Why custom code instead of a page-builder template?+

Custom development gives us greater control over design, performance, structure and future expansion. We use the approach that best serves the project rather than forcing the project into a tool's limitations.

How long does a website take to build?+

Timelines vary based on scope, content readiness and functionality. Discovery establishes a clear project plan and expected timeline before development begins.

Will my website work well on mobile?+

Yes. Mobile is intentionally designed—not treated as a smaller desktop site. Navigation, spacing, typography, imagery and conversion paths are adapted for the screen.

Do you build SEO into the website?+

Yes. Technical SEO, semantic structure, metadata, performance, internal linking and appropriate structured data are considered as part of the build.

Can you replace my existing website?+

Yes. We can evaluate what should be preserved, improved or rebuilt and create a stronger production foundation without assuming everything from the old site has to be discarded.

Can the website grow into an app, portal or larger platform later?+

Yes. When future expansion is likely, we plan for that direction so the website can become part of a larger connected digital ecosystem.

Do you only work with large companies?+

No. We work with local businesses, churches, organizations and growing companies as well as larger opportunities. The goal is to build the right solution for the actual need.
