COMMON QUESTIONS

Before We
Build.

Every website is different. These are some of the questions we hear most often.

How much does a custom website cost?+

It depends on the scope, content, functionality, integrations and complexity. We recommend the right-sized solution after understanding what the website needs to accomplish rather than forcing every business into the same package.

How long does a website take to build?+

Timelines vary by project size, content readiness and functionality. During discovery we establish a clear project scope and expected timeline before development begins.

Will my website work well on mobile?+

Yes. Mobile is intentionally designed, not simply a smaller version of desktop. We refine layout, navigation, spacing, typography and conversion paths for smaller screens.

Do you build SEO into the website?+

Yes. Technical SEO, semantic structure, metadata, performance, internal linking and appropriate structured data are considered during the build rather than treated as an afterthought.

Can you work with a business outside Florida?+

Absolutely. Eagle Vision Digital is based in Fort Walton Beach, Florida and serves businesses across the United States and Canada.

Can my website grow into an app, portal or larger platform later?+

Yes. One of our core goals is to avoid unnecessary dead ends. When future expansion is likely, we plan the website so it can become part of a broader connected digital ecosystem.
