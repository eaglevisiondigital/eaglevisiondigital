FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Before You Build
Your Mobile App.

The questions worth answering before you invest in something your customers will carry with them.

How much does a custom mobile app cost?+

Investment depends on functionality, integrations, user roles, content, workflows and support needs. We recommend the right-sized approach after understanding what the app needs to accomplish.

Do you build for both iPhone and Android?+

Yes. We can plan mobile experiences for both iOS and Android so users have a consistent branded experience across supported devices.

Do we need an app if we already have a website?+

Not necessarily. Websites are generally strongest for discovery and acquisition. Apps become especially valuable when customers, members or teams have a reason to return, engage, transact or access information repeatedly.

Can the app connect with our website?+

Yes. The website and app can be planned as connected parts of the same digital experience when that serves the business.

Can the app accept payments or orders?+

Yes. Depending on the use case, apps can support commerce, ordering, deposits, donations, subscriptions, booking and other transaction flows.

Can we send push notifications?+

Yes. Notifications can be used for reminders, communication, promotions, content and other timely engagement when appropriate to the experience.

Can customers have their own login or profile?+

Yes. Secure accounts, profiles, saved preferences, history, memberships and role-based access can be part of the app strategy.

Can the app include chat, courses or member content?+

Yes. Messaging, courses, media, downloads, progress tracking and member-only experiences can be included based on the project.

What if we are not sure which features we need?+

That is exactly what discovery is for. We start with the business goal and the user behavior you want to create, then determine which features actually support that outcome.

Can the app expand later?+

Yes. We intentionally think about future growth so the experience can evolve without assuming you need every feature on day one.
