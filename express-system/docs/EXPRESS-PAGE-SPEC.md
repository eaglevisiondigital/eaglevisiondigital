# Eagle Vision Digital `/express` Page Spec

## Positioning

Primary customer-facing product name: **Express Custom Websites**.

Do not lead with “AI website.” AI may be explained as one part of the production engine after the value proposition is established.

Core promise:

**Custom Built. No Templates. Ready for Review in 3–24 Hours.**

Supporting app promise:

**Add an Express Custom App. Initial build ready for review and device testing in 3–7 business days. Apple/Google review time is separate.**

The page must fit the live Eagle Vision Digital brand: premium dark presentation, blue glow accents, glass/card treatments, confident typography, clean motion, strong responsive behavior, and the existing emphasis on custom digital solutions built to help businesses grow.

## Suggested route

`/express`

This keeps the product expandable beyond websites into Website + App.

## Hero

Eyebrow: `EAGLE VISION EXPRESS`

H1: `Custom Built. No Templates. Ready for Review in 3–24 Hours.`

Body: `A professionally designed Eagle Vision Digital website custom-composed around your business, brand, customers and goals. Our Express production system combines strategic intake, reusable engineering, AI-assisted production and human quality control to deliver custom work at a dramatically faster pace.`

Primary CTA: `START MY EXPRESS BUILD`

Secondary CTA: `SEE HOW EXPRESS WORKS`

Trust line: `Website • App • Website + App`

## Key explanation

Headline: `Custom Doesn't Have to Mean Slow.`

Explain that Eagle Vision reuses proven engineering and industry architecture, not the client’s identity, content, composition or brand presentation.

Preferred line:

**We reuse proven engineering where it makes sense. We do not reuse your identity.**

## Three product cards

### Express Website
- Standard five-page custom website
- Industry-specific page architecture
- Responsive desktop/mobile
- Professional copy generation from verified facts
- Brand-aligned custom composition
- Initial standard-scope build ready for review in 3–24 hours after acceptance

### Express App
- Custom branded app shell and navigation
- Selected certified native features
- Shared website/app content where practical
- Initial standard-scope build ready for review/device testing in 3–7 business days
- App-store review time excluded

### Website + App
- One strategic intake
- Shared information model
- Coordinated brand and calls to action
- Website and app designed as one digital presence

## How Express works

1. Tell us about your business in a short adaptive intake.
2. Eagle Vision validates scope, facts and required assets.
3. Our production system generates the first build.
4. Eagle Vision performs QA and internal review.
5. You review and approve the version.
6. We launch after final approval.

## AI positioning

Use later on the page, not in the product title:

`AI-assisted production helps Eagle Vision move faster, but every Express project is governed by your verified information, your brand, Eagle Vision design standards and human quality control.`

Avoid wording that implies a one-click generic AI site generator.

## Guarantee / scope language

Recommended customer-facing wording:

`Once Eagle Vision has received, reviewed and accepted the complete information and required assets for a standard-scope Express website, the initial full website build is guaranteed to be ready for Eagle Vision review and client preview within 3–24 hours.`

`Standard-scope Express app builds are targeted for review and device testing within 3–7 business days after acceptance. Apple App Store and Google Play review, developer-account enrollment and third-party approval timelines are separate.`

Add scope protection immediately nearby:

`Advanced custom software, extensive ecommerce, large databases, unsupported third-party integrations, unusual migration requirements or other nonstandard development may require a custom project scope. Eagle Vision will identify that before the Express timing commitment begins.`

## Site integration

Add Express as a visible path near the existing Custom Websites / Custom Mobile Apps offerings, but do not replace the broader custom-development pages. Express is a delivery product inside Eagle Vision Digital, not a new brand.

Recommended navigation treatment: `Express` or `Express Build`.

Recommended internal cross-links:
- Home -> Express
- Custom Websites -> Express CTA
- Custom Mobile Apps -> Website + App Express CTA
- Website OR App / comparison content -> Express bundle CTA
- Free Website promotion -> Express intake after qualification
