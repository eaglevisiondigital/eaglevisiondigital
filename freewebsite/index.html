STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Free Website Offer FAQ

Is the website really free?

Yes, for businesses approved for this promotional program, the normal upfront website-development charge may be waived for the approved custom website project. Specialized third-party services, ongoing services or functionality outside the approved promotional scope may carry separate costs, but anything outside the included scope will be explained before you agree to move forward.

Are templates used?

No. This promotion is specifically for a custom-designed Eagle Vision Digital website developed around your business, brand, customers and goals.

Is this a basic free website?

No. The goal is to provide qualifying businesses with a professionally designed digital presence. The exact scope is defined during the qualification and planning process.

Do I have to process at least $20,000 per month?

The full free custom website promotion is designed primarily for businesses processing approximately $20,000 or more per month in credit/debit card sales. Businesses below that level may still qualify for another digital-upgrade incentive.

What if I process less than $20,000 per month?

You may still qualify for up to $1,000 toward a custom website or mobile app, depending on your business, processing volume and project scope. Submit the eligibility form and we will review the available options.

Do I automatically qualify if I process $20,000 per month?

No. Processing volume is one qualification factor. Business type, processing profile, underwriting, project requirements and approved scope may also affect eligibility.

Do I have to switch credit card processors?

Submitting the eligibility form does not obligate you to switch processors. Eagle Vision Payments will first review your current setup and explain the available options. You decide whether moving forward makes sense for your business.

Why is payment processing connected to the website offer?

Eagle Vision Digital and Eagle Vision Payments work together to create long-term business relationships. For qualifying merchants, that relationship may allow us to offset the normal upfront development cost of a custom website.

What if I already have a website?

That's fine. We can evaluate whether your current website should be redesigned, upgraded or replaced.

What if my website needs are unusually complex?

Most traditional local business websites can fit within the promotional program. If your project requires advanced custom software, extensive ecommerce, large databases, specialized integrations or other unusually complex development, we will review those requirements with you before you agree to anything. You will know exactly what is included before moving forward.

Will you try to upsell me after I qualify?

The promotional website scope will be clearly defined before you make a payment-processing decision. If something you request falls outside the included promotional scope, we will tell you upfront. There are no surprise website-development charges after the fact.

Do I have to upload my processing statement with the form?

No. Uploading a statement is optional. Providing one can speed up the eligibility review, but you may submit the initial form without it.

What information do you review on the statement?

We primarily review information such as processing volume, processor, rates, fees and payment structure so we can determine whether your business fits the promotional program and whether there may be opportunities to improve your payment setup.

How does the free mobile app opportunity work?

Some qualifying businesses may also be eligible for a custom mobile app depending on processing volume, business needs and approved project scope. Eagle Vision will explain app eligibility during the review.

Is the promotion available everywhere?

This particular promotional campaign is being offered to qualifying businesses in the Emerald Coast / Northwest Florida market.

When does the offer end?

The promotion expires September 29th or when all 20 available custom website build slots have been claimed.
